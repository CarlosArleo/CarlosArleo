
# Carlos Arleo

Architect and researcher, and founder of Localis. I build tools that help people examine planning decisions and public data.

## Work

- **[Your Planning Voice](https://yourplanningvoice.com/landing)**: helps community groups draft planning objections grounded in planning policy.
- **[Darwinian Novelty Indicator](https://dni-5-0-functions.vercel.app/dni)**: assesses how novel a piece of research is by comparing it with retrieved prior work.
- **[Ariadne](https://ariadne-atlas.vercel.app/)**: screens drug-repurposing hypotheses for rare diseases, with human review.
- **[Constitutional Physics](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19616290)**: preprints on AI systems that stop when their rules can’t be met.
